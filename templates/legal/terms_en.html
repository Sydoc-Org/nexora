Accounts

An account is personal. It may only be used by the person it was opened for, and only for that person's work for their organisation.

Careful use

Do not share your password, do not let anyone work in your session, and keep your second sign-in factor on a device only you use. Contact Sydoc as soon as you suspect either has fallen into someone else's hands.

Do not try to reach data you have no permission for, or to get around security measures. Do not put nexora under excessive load, for example with mass automated requests, and do not introduce malicious software. Only upload content you are entitled to and that is not unlawful, for example as a profile picture or as an attachment to a feedback message.
